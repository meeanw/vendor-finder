How picks are chosen

How picks are chosen: a hand-built shortlist, not a ranking by sales or stock. For each room and furniture type there are one or more style slots (40 slots across 23 room/type groups), usually 3 vendors per slot.

112 of 117 picks share a style with the slot (using the styles in this file). Only 69 of the 162 vendors appear in Quick Picks; use All Furniture Matches or Vendor Directory for the rest. Picks do not check item-level stock or price.
